Danone × Alpro

Creating and statistically validating a plant-based "Fizzy Protein Yogurt Soda Mix" tailored for Gen Z and Young Millennials.

864

Combinations Evaluated

39.7M

Year 1 Revenue Scenario

Winning Profile & Research Finding

Yuzu Lychee Instant Sachet (15g Protein / 3g Sugar)

Conjoint analysis revealed that flavour and price were stronger decision drivers than protein content alone.

Pricing Experiment Finding: "Try first. Bundle later." Single sachets at €1.20 scored a 73% purchase likelihood, whereas 3-sachet bundles at €3.50 saw a steep drop-off (Cohen's d = .890, p < .001).
